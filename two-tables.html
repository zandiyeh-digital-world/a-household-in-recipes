Transcription, interpretation & limits

Recommended menus printed in the book, not records of meals actually eaten. Family days and servants’ group sizes are independent choices; the book does not pair them as one observed household. Line breaks and typography are normalised. No class, income, family size, meal price or recipe identity is inferred from a menu heading alone.

The 21 daily family/little-dinner entries were checked against printed p. 1407; the five servants’ menus against printed p. 1408. Both source images are included. These two leaves have PDF sequence pages 1517–1518, while the downloaded page-number index identifies Archive leaves 1521–1522; printed page and local scan are the primary references. Prices shown lower on p. 1408 belong to the vegetarian menus, not the servants’ meals, and are not attached to this comparison. ‘Salmon shad’ and ‘crême’ are preserved as printed. Course group breaks follow the printed em dashes; ingredient chips and highlights are editorial aids, not additional historical classifications.

The source is University of Leeds Library’s copy of The Book of Household Management, Internet Archive b21536193, catalogued as 1899. Its preface is dated November 1888. Scans are credited to the University of Leeds Library / Internet Archive, whose item record carries a Public Domain Mark.
